## Goal

Configure daily, weekly, and monthly recurrence and expose safe occurrence-versus-series edits.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Task details exposes one-time, daily, weekly, and monthly choices and shows the saved choice when reopened.
- [ ] Saving a recurrence choice creates/updates the correct template and results in expected future occurrences without duplicating an already generated occurrence.
- [ ] For a recurring task, edit/delete actions make the one-occurrence versus whole-series scope explicit and follow the approved #2 rules.
- [ ] Canceling a scoped action leaves the template and all occurrences unchanged; a failed save does not partially alter the series.
- [ ] Examples on screen or in a targeted report cover a missing monthly date and an unfinished earlier occurrence; prior completions are preserved according to #2.
- [ ] Changing recurrence refreshes affected views and persists across restart without enabling accounts or custom recurrence frequencies.

## Out of scope

- Generation algorithm is tracked in [#13](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/13).
- Generic single-item delete/undo flow is tracked in [#27](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/27).
- Reminder configuration is tracked in [#29](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/29).
- Background execution is tracked in [#31](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/31).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #12, #13, and #17; recurrence scope rules from #2.
- Files/scope: Recurrence controls and scoped task-details actions, their repository integration, and targeted checks under app.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
