## Goal

Configure daily, weekly, or monthly recurrence in real task details, and safely edit, pause/resume, or delete one occurrence or its whole series. Saved choices drive offline generation and remain visible after reopening.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opening a real task row shows its saved One time, Daily, Weekly, or Monthly choice; a recurring occurrence also shows enabled/paused state, the series anchor, and its original slot date if its displayed date was moved. The frequency/date chooser previews the next nominal dates, including a clamped monthly date, without creating future active records. Closing/reopening and offline restart retain the choice, including completed or overridden occurrences.
- [ ] A dated ordinary task can become a daily, weekly, or monthly series on Save, anchored to its selected date. An undated task cannot start recurrence and retains its draft with an actionable date error. Conversion preserves the selected task ID, content, checklist and completion state, attaches it to one original slot, and reconciles due slots without duplicating that slot or creating future active records early. Invalid input or failure leaves all saved data unchanged.
- [ ] Editing a recurring task makes **this occurrence** versus **whole series** an explicit choice before Save. This occurrence changes only the chosen task, preserving immutable original slot/series identity when its date, list, content, reminder or checklist changes; the template, other slots and exclusions are unchanged. Completion/reopening and checklist behavior follow #2/#18. Cancel/dismiss discards the draft.
- [ ] A series Save can change its frequency, start/anchor date, list, title, priority, note, checklist prototype and applicable existing reminder value. Apply #2 prospectively from the current local day even when invoked from historical/completed records: preserve completed history, past unfinished tasks, overrides and exclusions; update eligible unmodified unfinished tasks displayed today for content edits; replace eligible current/future unmodified slots for schedule changes. A changed schedule starts today or later. Existing and excluded original slots cannot be duplicated, including moved or completed slots. Failure rolls back the whole Save and keeps the draft for correction.
- [ ] Choosing One time on a recurring task explicitly pauses that series after confirmation, retaining existing occurrences and completed history. Its details show Paused and offer Resume of the same frequency/anchor; resume reconciles missed due slots exactly once. Canceling pause/resume changes nothing. A One time ordinary task remains ordinary.
- [ ] Recurring Delete explicitly offers **this occurrence** and **entire series**, each with permanent-deletion confirmation. The former removes the selected occurrence and reserves its original slot from regeneration. The latter removes the template, all occurrences including completed history and moved overrides, and exclusions. Cancel leaves everything intact; failure is atomic. Ordinary task deletion remains #27.
- [ ] Successful Save, pause/resume and confirmed deletion refresh the visible row/affected real app views without restarting and persist through offline restart. Repository writes run off the main thread; pending writes prevent duplicate submission/dismissal; errors are visible and retryable; concurrent deletion of task/list/series cannot resurrect data. The sheet keeps actions reachable with keyboard, long content and large text; scope and confirmation controls have clear accessible labels.
- [ ] Targeted checks and `_docs/task-19-validation.md` cover real `MainActivity` row-to-details behavior and saved Room state: unfinished older daily occurrence plus due new slot; monthly Jan 31 → Feb 28/29 → Mar 31 without anchor drift; weekly weekday; completed/moved occurrence versus series edit; cancel/failure; pause/resume; one-slot exclusion and whole-series deletion; same-day refresh and offline restart. Compare IDs, slots, completion, overrides, revisions and exclusions as relevant. Record commands, tested commit, device/API and any unavailable device checks.

## Constraints

- PM choice under the owner's 2026-10-12 routine-decision delegation: One time on a recurring task pauses its template instead of detaching/deleting occurrences; Resume restores the same series. Example: pause an Oct 12 daily series on Oct 14; Oct 12 completed and Oct 13 overdue remain, Oct 15 is not generated while paused; resume Oct 16 reconciles Oct 15/16 once. This is a delegated choice, not an individual owner selection. Keep pause/delete consequences explicit, and use #17/#18 explicit Save for editable fields.
