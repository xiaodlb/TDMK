## Goal

Resolve task behavior choices needed by the data model and implementation, recording explicitly approved or owner-delegated rules in _docs/behavior.md.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A task-rules table records the approved default priority, equal-priority tie-breaker, and dedicated Upcoming date range with examples for each.
- [ ] The document records parent/subtask completion, task reopening, and Inbox rename/delete protections with explicit before/after examples.
- [ ] Daily/weekly/monthly recurrence rules specify missing monthly dates and editing/deleting one occurrence versus a series, including already completed occurrences.
- [ ] Reminder rules cover missed/past times, overdue tasks, changed or removed dates, clock/timezone changes, and notification/exact-alarm permission denial; priority remains manually selected and does not trigger repeated alerts.
- [ ] Every required policy is recorded with a concrete example and an approval source: explicit owner selection or a choice made under the owner's 2026-10-12 delegation to decide the remaining policies autonomously. Previously selected answers remain unchanged; delegated choices are not described as individually selected by the owner.
- [ ] The task section preserves the settled scope: date-only tasks, High/Medium/Low, overdue retention, Monday-start weeks, completed-today visibility, and schedule-based recurrence.
