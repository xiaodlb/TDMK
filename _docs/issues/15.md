## Goal

Provide a minimal light/dark theme with persisted System, Light, and Dark choices.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Settings exposes System, Light, and Dark options; System is the initial default and the selected option is visibly indicated.
- [ ] Selecting Light or Dark updates every currently implemented destination immediately; restarting retains the manual choice.
- [ ] Selecting System follows phone appearance and responds to a system light/dark change without requiring a reinstall.
- [ ] Neutral colors, typography, and spacing remain consistent across tabs and settings in both modes, with readable contrast and visible focus/selection.
- [ ] The setting is stored locally and works without an account or network; checks record mode switching and restart behavior.

## Out of scope

- Full accessibility review is tracked in [#37](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/37).
- Dashboard content is tracked in [#22](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/22).
- Formatted-note editor is tracked in [#25](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/25).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #14 and settings representation from #4.
- Files/scope: Shared Compose theme/tokens, appearance settings UI/local persistence, and focused checks under app; no full design-system library.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
