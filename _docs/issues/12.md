## Goal

Persist simple recurrence templates, revisions, occurrence identities and exclusions for reliable later generation, integrating existing task/subtask/list mutations with the resolved series ownership rules.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Daily/weekly/monthly templates retain all #4 fields after on-disk reopening: stable globally unique ID, owning-list FK, enabled flag/timestamps and nonempty ordered revisions with number, inclusive/exclusive boundaries, original anchor/frequency, content/reminder/checklist prototypes and local reconciledThrough cursor. Same-day empty superseded revisions and older provenance are retained; prototype order/text are valid and separate from actual task subtasks. Persist known occurrence writes/cursor advancement transactionally without advancing after a failed batch; automatic schedule enumeration remains #13.
- [ ] A recurring occurrence stores paired seriesId/immutable original slotDate/sourceRevision and isOverride; ordinary tasks retain null recurrence fields and false override. Enforce series FK RESTRICT and unique (seriesId, slotDate), plus exclusion composite identity/disjointness: one original slot can have a live occurrence or an exclusion, never both, across schedule revisions. Edited display date, task UUID, timezone and completion do not change slot identity; two distinct slots may share a displayed date. Validate original-slot schedule provenance against the retained revision rather than rejecting preserved slots solely because its cutoff changed.
- [ ] Template/occurrence CRUD implements #2/#4's single-versus-series rules through all existing task/subtask mutation paths: a one-occurrence edit or reopen preserves its slot and marks its override without changing others; one-occurrence deletion atomically removes its children and reserves its original slot with an exclusion. Prospective series edits append/close revisions from today's local day, update only eligible today's uncompleted/unmodified records, and preserve completed history, past unfinished records and overrides/exclusions. Schedule changes require a new anchor today or later and remove superseded unmodified unfinished slots without adding user-deletion exclusions; content-only edits retain anchor/frequency. Checklist prototypes create fresh unchecked children where applicable, preserving #8's parent gate/automatic reopening and #11 completion days.
- [ ] Disabling a series retains all occurrences/revisions/exclusions; enabling retains the anchor/provenance. Series deletion atomically removes the template, every occurrence (including completed history/moved overrides), children and exclusions. Custom-list deletion now integrates the full ownership contract: delete templates owned by that list and their occurrences even after moving to another list; for tasks in the deleted list belonging to a surviving externally owned series, reserve their original slots before deletion. Preserve unrelated lists/tasks/notes and default-list protection, compact order, and verify rollback on an injected failure with no partial records/exclusions.
- [ ] Focused real API 36 persistence checks cover daily/weekly/monthly metadata and monthly clamp provenance, revisions/same-day edits, future-start series content/schedule edits before their anchor (initial boundary min(anchorDate, creation local day), with no early slots), moved/undated/completed overrides, slot collisions/exclusions, single/series/list deletion, disabling, invalid frequency/anchor/revision/prototype/FK values, unknown IDs and duplicate/reserved-slot insert outcomes. Add/register non-destructive Room 5-to-6 migration with generated schema 6, retain schemas 1–5/full chain, and validate exported schema-5 upgrade preserving every existing Settings/list/task/subtask/note field/ID/completion/formatting before further recurrence writes/reopen. Record device/commands/results and run relevant existing checks/debug build; no automatic generation, UI or OS alarm behavior is claimed.
