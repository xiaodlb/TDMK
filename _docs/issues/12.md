## Goal

Persist simple recurrence templates and occurrence identities for reliable later generation.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Daily, weekly, and monthly templates retain frequency, anchor, relationship, and lifecycle metadata from #4 after database reopening.
- [ ] Each ordinary occurrence references its template and scheduled-date identity; uniqueness prevents two records for the same template/date.
- [ ] Template and occurrence create/read/update/delete operations respect the single-occurrence versus whole-series policy from #2.
- [ ] Deleting or disabling a series preserves or removes past/completed occurrences exactly as the approved policy states, without unexplained orphan references.
- [ ] Invalid frequency/anchor, unknown template IDs, and duplicate occurrence identities are rejected or handled idempotently with a targeted persistence report.

## Out of scope

- Occurrence-generation algorithm is tracked in [#13](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/13).
- Recurrence UI is tracked in [#19](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/19).
- Background reconciliation is tracked in [#31](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/31).
- Reminder scheduling is tracked in [#28](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/28).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #4 and #7; recurrence edit/delete rules from #2.
- Files/scope: Recurrence entities/DAOs/repositories, necessary task references/constraints, schemas, and focused persistence checks.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.

Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
