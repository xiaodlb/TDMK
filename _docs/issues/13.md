## Goal

Generate daily, weekly, and monthly occurrences on schedule, independently of older task completion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fixed-date fixture shows expected daily, weekly, and monthly occurrences from their anchors, including month/year transitions.
- [ ] An unfinished prior occurrence remains present and overdue when the next scheduled occurrence is generated.
- [ ] Running reconciliation twice for the same template and range produces the same occurrence IDs/count and no duplicate tasks.
- [ ] Reopening after missed days reconciles the agreed missed range and uses the missing-monthly-date policy from #2.
- [ ] Occurrence-versus-series exclusions/edits/deletions from #2 are respected; a deliberately deleted occurrence is not unintentionally recreated.
- [ ] A targeted report shows deterministic results using a controllable clock and the occurrence uniqueness contract rather than relying on exact background execution.

## Out of scope

- Recurrence controls is tracked in [#19](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/19).
- Background execution is tracked in [#31](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/31).
- Reminder restoration/reconciliation is tracked in [#30](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/30).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #12; recurrence/date rules from #2 and #4.
- Files/scope: Recurrence domain generator and repository reconciliation under app plus boundary/idempotency checks; no scheduling service or UI.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
