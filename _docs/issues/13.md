## Goal

Generate daily, weekly, and monthly occurrences on schedule, independently of older task completion.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Fixed-date fixtures enumerate daily anchor+n days, weekly anchor+7n days and monthly original-anchor-day slots across month/year transitions. January 31 yields February 28 (February 29 in leap years), March 31 and April 30 without changing the anchor. Only slots due through captured current-device today are materialized; a future anchor has no early occurrence despite its corrected earlier initial effectiveFrom.
- [ ] An unfinished prior occurrence remains unchanged/overdue when the next due occurrence is generated, independent of previous completion. New records copy the applicable revision's title/priority/note/requested reminder/checklist and current template list, with fresh stable task/child IDs, completion fields null, isOverride=false and every child unchecked; previous checklist/completion states are never carried forward.
- [ ] Replaying reconciliation through the same day, reopening and retrying a failed batch preserve occurrence IDs/count and never duplicate a (seriesId, original slotDate) reservation. Use persisted cursor plus transactional uniqueness/exclusion checks, not cursor alone; occurrence/children writes and cursor advancement commit together. A targeted injected failure leaves the failed batch's rows/cursor unchanged while already committed batches remain usable.
- [ ] Reopening after missed days reconciles every eligible missed nominal date across retained nonempty revision intervals through today, with no rolling lookback cap or discarded gaps; batching may bound work but must resume to cover the full range. Example: daily October 12 remains overdue, reopening October 15 adds exactly October 13/14/15. Advance cursors through processed nonslot days; backward clock/day changes do not erase cursor/reservations, and a forward jump catches up once. Reset-cursor replay (the restore contract) preserves existing records/exclusions rather than regenerating them.
- [ ] Generation respects #12's revisions/boundaries, overrides and exclusions across the stable series: a deleted original slot stays absent, moved/undated/completed records reserve their original slot, distinct original slots may share a displayed date, and a revised schedule cannot duplicate a preserved reservation. Same-day empty revisions generate nothing; future-start content/schedule edits follow the corrected initial boundary. Disabled/deleted series generate nothing, re-enabled series reconcile retained gaps without resetting anchor, and series/list deletion cannot recreate removed storage. Preserve prospective edits, completion history and past unfinished occurrences exactly as #2/#4 specify.
- [ ] Focused deterministic unit and real API 36 on-disk reconciliation tests/report cover calendar fixtures, missed-range/reopen, repeated/reset-cursor replay, revision collisions/overrides/exclusions, disabled/re-enabled/deleted series and failure rollback using a controllable clock/date source and existing schema-6 storage. Record device/API/commands/results and run relevant existing tests/debug build. Catch-up persists requested past-reminder metadata without posting/replaying notifications; OS reminder delivery/rebuild tests remain #28/#30, not a claim of exact background execution here.
