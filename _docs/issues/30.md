## Goal

Reconcile pending task reminders after reboot, application launch, clock/timezone changes, and permission changes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After a device/emulator reboot, eligible future reminders are restored and completed/deleted tasks remain unscheduled.
- [ ] Application launch reconciles recurring occurrences and their pending reminders without duplicate alarms or duplicate notifications.
- [ ] Manual clock/timezone changes apply #2/#4's date/time interpretation and replace outdated alarms; date-only task dates do not shift accidentally.
- [ ] Missed/past reminders follow the approved policy, with a targeted result showing that reconciliation does not flood stale notifications.
- [ ] Revoking and restoring notification/exact-alarm permissions yields the documented status/fallback on the next reconciliation rather than silently losing the schedule.
- [ ] A recorded device/emulator check covers reboot, clock change, and repeated reconciliation with stable alarm identities.

## Out of scope

- Initial scheduling engine is tracked in [#28](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/28).
- Periodic background reconciliation is tracked in [#31](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/31).
- Foreground screen date refresh is tracked in [#35](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/35).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #13 and #28; clock/timezone/missed-reminder policies from #2 and #4.
- Files/scope: Reboot/clock receivers and reminder reconciliation hooks under app, manifest registration, and focused device checks.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
