## Goal

Persist ordered subtasks and independent check states for a parent task.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create, edit, reorder, check/uncheck, and delete operations affect the intended subtask and retain its stable identity when appropriate.
- [ ] A multi-item checklist preserves text, order, and checked states after database reopening.
- [ ] Completing or reopening a parent applies exactly the parent/subtask policy approved in #2; examples show both mixed and fully checked lists.
- [ ] Deleting a parent removes its subtasks atomically, and adding a subtask to a nonexistent parent is rejected without an orphan.
- [ ] Invalid text, duplicate ordering positions, unknown IDs, and write failures follow the documented validation/ordering rules, with check results.

## Out of scope

- Checklist UI is tracked in [#18](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/18).
- Completed-task date visibility is tracked in [#11](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/11).
- Generating recurring occurrences is tracked in [#13](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/13).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #7 and the subtask relationship/completion rules from #2 and #4.
- Files/scope: Subtask storage/repository and necessary parent-completion integration under app plus relevant schema and persistence checks.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
