## Goal

Schedule and cancel local reminders for dated tasks using Android alarms and notifications with explicit permission handling.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A saved dated task with a permitted future reminder schedules exactly one identifiable local alarm and notification channel; an undated task cannot schedule an alarm.
- [ ] On a real Android device with required permissions, the configured reminder produces the expected task notification and the device/API/time result is recorded.
- [ ] Completing or deleting the task cancels its pending alarm, and canceled tasks do not notify when the former trigger time arrives.
- [ ] Notification denial, channel disablement, unavailable exact-alarm access, and a past trigger apply #2's documented fallback rather than silently claiming an exact reminder is active.
- [ ] Scheduling the same task twice is idempotent, and updating a scheduled reminder cancels its old alarm before replacing it.
- [ ] No cloud push service or priority-driven repeat alerts are introduced; alarm failures are visible to callers.

## Out of scope

- User-facing reminder controls is tracked in [#29](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/29).
- Reboot/timezone restoration is tracked in [#30](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/30).
- Recurring background work is tracked in [#31](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/31).
