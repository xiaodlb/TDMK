## Goal

Persist completion times and derive same-day crossed-out visibility and older completion history without a midnight database move.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Completing a task stores its completion timestamp once and makes it appear completed in domain results; repeating the completion operation does not create another history record.
- [ ] For a fixed local day, a task completed that day remains in its applicable active views as completed, while earlier completions appear in historical results and no longer appear in active views.
- [ ] History groups use completion day, newest first, including a task originally scheduled for another date.
- [ ] Reopening follows the policy from #2, retains the task identity, and updates active/history results consistently with its date and subtasks.
- [ ] A deterministic result table/report covers just-before/after-midnight completion, app restart on a later day, clock/date changes, and timezone interpretation from #4.
- [ ] No background job is required to physically move completed records into a separate log table.

## Out of scope

- Log UI is tracked in [#23](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/23).
- Crossed-out row rendering is tracked in [#16](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/16).
- Screen refresh across foreground date changes is tracked in [#35](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/35).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #7 and #10; completion/reopening rules from #2 and timestamp contract from #4.
- Files/scope: Completion/reopening repository/domain logic and relevant deterministic/persistence checks under app; no screen design.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.

Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
