## Goal

Document the local data model for TDMK in `_docs/data-model.md` so an engineer can implement its offline Room/SQLite storage without guessing field meanings, relationships, or lifecycle rules. Cover tasks, lists, subtasks, independent formatted notes, settings, and recurring templates with their occurrences; do not implement storage or cloud sync.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `_docs/data-model.md` contains an entity/relationship diagram or table covering lists, tasks, subtasks, notes, settings, and recurring templates; each relationship states its cardinality and ownership, and notes have no relationship to tasks.
- [ ] Every persisted field has a name, type/encoding, nullability, default or an explicit statement that it has no default, and validation rule; the tables cover list order, task title/list/date/priority/short note/reminder/completion, subtask text/order/completion, note title/body/search text/timestamps, and the agreed settings and recurrence metadata. Reminder configuration and consumed-attempt identity/state, retained delivery-safety metadata, and durable reconciliation state are explicit, including the union of snapshot/current-device consumed attempts on replacement restore without merging task/note data.
- [ ] The document specifies stable-ID generation and uniqueness, protected but renamable default Inbox identity, foreign-key behavior, and any uniqueness constraints; an occurrence has an immutable original slot identity within its stable series, independent of edited display date, completion, timezone, and schedule revision. Document how reconciliation across revisions avoids duplicates and preserves excluded slots; distinct slots may share a displayed date.
- [ ] Scheduled dates, reminder times, and completion/creation/update timestamps each have a stated representation and timezone interpretation; examples show an undated task, a dated task without a reminder, a dated task with a reminder, completion across midnight, and travel to another timezone without changing a date-only task's scheduled day.
- [ ] A lifecycle table states what is stored or derived for active, overdue, completed-today, historically completed, reopened, and deleted tasks; it includes the parent/subtask completion gate and automatic reopening, permanent confirmed deletion without trash/undo, deletion of a populated list including its series/moved overrides, and reminder cancellation. Completion timestamps/days and retained checklist/date state match #2; no midnight database move is required.
- [ ] Recurrence tables distinguish templates from occurrences and contain enough metadata to resume generation after an app restart, including anchor, schedule revisions/boundaries, original slots, overrides, and deletion exclusions; examples cover an unfinished previous occurrence, replayed reconciliation, a missed interval, a monthly date absent from a month, and occurrence-versus-series edits/deletions using #2. Include revision collision with preserved completed/overridden/excluded slots, disabling repetition, and catch-up after restore without regeneration of deleted slots.
- [ ] The note contract names its versioned formatting representation, plain-text search/preview representation, and policy for invalid or unsupported format versions; examples include an empty body, bold text, headings, bullet lists, and Unicode, with no images or attachments. Specify saved-note timestamps/order and #3's new-blank versus existing-cleared behavior. Define derived shadow-text encoding/rebuild for every searchable field using #3's NFKC, default full non-Turkic case fold, NFKC, and Unicode-whitespace matching pipeline; include Straße/STRASSE and composed/decomposed accent examples, preserve original text/formatting, and do not rely on SQLite ASCII-only case matching.
- [ ] A schema/format-versioning section identifies what is versioned and documents how existing IDs, relationships, completion timestamps, and note formatting are preserved during future schema changes; stable local IDs do not require accounts or server IDs. Define the logical version-1 UTF-8 JSON backup envelope/payload mapping and separately versioned rich-note schema, included appearance settings and excluded derived/transient/OS data, cross-record validation, supported-version rejection, and #3's size/record limits; explain atomic record/settings replacement with retained reminder safety and durable reconciliation, without implementing export/restore or migrations.
- [ ] An annotated example dataset contains Inbox, a custom list, an undated task, a dated task with a subtask and reminder, a completed task, a recurring template with two distinct occurrences, a formatted note, and settings; every field and reference matches the documented tables.
- [ ] The document links the scope and the completed decision record from #2 and #3; its behavior-sensitive rules match those decisions, and no implementation-blocking default, deletion policy, recurrence rule, or note-format choice is left as an unexplained TODO. Record any remaining contract choices and examples as made under the owner's 2026-10-12 delegation, preserving explicit owner selections and resolved policies.
