## Goal

Persist independent formatted notes and human-readable preview/search content.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create/read/update/delete preserve note ID, title, timestamps, and formatted body according to the note contract after reopening.
- [ ] Bold, headings, bullet lists, Unicode, and an empty body round-trip without formatting loss or crashes.
- [ ] Stored search/preview content is readable plain text and updates with the formatted body rather than exposing serialization markup.
- [ ] Invalid or unsupported formatting versions follow the documented policy and are not silently overwritten with corrupted content.
- [ ] Notes have no required task/list relationships, and deleting a task/list leaves unrelated notes intact.
- [ ] Targeted persistence results cover note update, deletion, independent ownership, and reopening.
