## Goal

Let the user choose an optional reminder time on a task's scheduled day from task details.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The task sheet exposes a reminder time only for a task with a date; an undated task explains that a date is required.
- [ ] Selecting/removing a reminder persists its state and scheduling status and remains correct after reopening the sheet/application.
- [ ] Enabling reminders explains and requests applicable permissions; denied access shows #2's fallback and does not falsely display successful exact scheduling.
- [ ] Changing a date/time or removing the date applies #2's rule, cancels the obsolete alarm, and leaves at most one valid replacement.
- [ ] Priority remains an independent Low/Medium/High selection and never auto-selects reminder times or repeated alerts.
- [ ] Canceling the time picker leaves the prior saved reminder unchanged; invalid/past times and scheduling errors have clear results.
