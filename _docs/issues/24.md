## Goal

Browse independent notes as a two-column card grid and provide creation/editor entry points.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Notes displays two columns of cards with title and plain-text preview in the note order approved in #3.
- [ ] New-note entry creates a note according to the agreed title/save policy, and selecting a card navigates to the note-editor route with the correct ID.
- [ ] Long titles/previews and empty bodies produce readable bounded cards without exposing formatting markup.
- [ ] A persisted note update or deletion is reflected immediately in the grid and ordering; demonstrate this through the repository without requiring the unfinished editor.
- [ ] Grid scrolling, larger text, keyboard transitions, and light/dark modes keep cards and entry controls usable.
- [ ] No folders, tags, task links, or image attachments are added.

## Out of scope

- Full-screen editing and formatting is tracked in [#25](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/25).
- Notes search is tracked in [#26](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/26).
- Delete/undo controls is tracked in [#27](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/27).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #9 and #14; note order/title policy from #3. Provide an editor route/entry contract without depending on #25; #25 supplies the editor content and verifies the complete return flow.
- Files/scope: Notes grid, navigation/creation entry points and repository observation under app plus targeted checks; editor content is #25.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
