## Goal

Let users manage custom lists and explicitly confirm deletion of a list and its tasks.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Manage lists opens from the final Tasks tab and exposes create, rename, reorder, and delete actions.
- [ ] Creating/renaming valid lists updates the tabs; invalid names display the approved validation and create no unusable list.
- [ ] Reordering custom lists persists after restart; fixed date-view tabs and Inbox retain their specified placement.
- [ ] Deleting a populated list displays a confirmation explaining that its tasks will also be deleted; canceling retains all records.
- [ ] Confirming deletion removes the list and its dependent task/subtask records atomically; failure does not leave partial data.
- [ ] Deleting the selected list selects a valid remaining tab, and Inbox actions follow #2's protections.

## Out of scope

- List persistence is tracked in [#6](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/6).
- Task persistence relationships is tracked in [#7](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/7).
- Deleting individual tasks or notes is tracked in [#27](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/27).
- Alarm cancellation integration is tracked in [#28](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/28).
