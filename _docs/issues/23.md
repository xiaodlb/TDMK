## Goal

Browse historical completions grouped by completion day and allow the agreed reopening action.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Log groups completed tasks by their completion day, newest day first, rather than scheduled date.
- [ ] Tasks completed today remain in active views and enter historical Log visibility on the next local day as specified by #11.
- [ ] Rows inside a group follow the agreed priority/tie-breaker ordering and open the existing task details sheet.
- [ ] Reopening a historical task follows #2's rules and immediately updates Log and the task's applicable active views without duplicating history.
- [ ] An empty Log, a long day group, and previously completed tasks after app restart/date change show clear results.

## Out of scope

- Log search is tracked in [#26](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/26).
- Completion storage/filtering rules is tracked in [#11](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/11).
- Foreground refresh mechanism is tracked in [#35](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/35).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #11, #14, #16, and #17; reopening rule from #2.
- Files/scope: Log Compose destination/grouping and existing task action integration under app plus relevant checks.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
