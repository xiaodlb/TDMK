## Goal

Make the real Tasks destination show its date views and saved lists in scrollable tabs. Task rows, completion, details and quick entry use the existing offline records, so changes appear in every applicable view without reopening the app.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Tasks destination has horizontally scrollable tabs in this order: Today, Upcoming, Anytime, the current name of the stable default Inbox, saved custom lists in their persisted order, then Manage lists. The first three labels and Manage lists stay fixed when Inbox is renamed; overflowing tabs can be reached and selected with touch and keyboard.
- [ ] Today shows tasks scheduled for the current local date across all lists; Upcoming shows dates from tomorrow through today + 7 local calendar days inclusive; Anytime shows undated tasks across all lists. Each list tab shows its own dated and undated tasks, including overdue tasks. An unfinished dated task from yesterday is absent from Today and remains in its list. For example, on October 12, a task dated October 19 is in Upcoming and one dated October 20 is outside it.
- [ ] Every tab uses the existing `TaskViews` result for its membership and ordering: High, Medium, Low; then oldest creation time; then stable ID ascending for exact ties. A task appears once per matching view. Displayed rows open the existing task-details sheet and expose the current task/list data; saved edits and recurrence occurrences update the matching tabs from Room without a restart.
- [ ] Completing a visible task updates its row to crossed out in each matching active tab for its captured completion day, subject to the existing completion gate. A completion from an earlier local day is absent from all Tasks tabs; reopening restores the task according to its retained date/list and subtask states. A completed-today task remains in its matching list and date view, including one with a future scheduled date.
- [ ] Quick entry on a custom-list or default-list tab assigns that list's stable ID; quick entry on Today, Upcoming or Anytime assigns the stable default-list ID even after Inbox is renamed. It retains the existing Medium default priority and undated default. The new task appears immediately in its list and Anytime; giving it a date through details moves it into the matching date view. Failed save leaves the draft and shows a retryable error; switching tabs does not silently submit or lose a draft.
- [ ] The selected tab survives switching main destinations and routine Activity recreation when its list still exists. If that list is removed, or no longer exists after a reload, Tasks selects a valid fixed tab and never shows a stale list or writes quick entry to a deleted ID. Tab selection and content remain usable after an offline process restart; saved lists/tasks retain their IDs and order.
- [ ] Empty results show a clear message while tabs and quick entry remain reachable. With many custom lists and long task results, both horizontal tabs and vertical content remain independently scrollable; navigation, bottom rows and entry controls are reachable in portrait and landscape at normal and enlarged text. Selected-tab, row, completion and entry controls have clear accessible labels/state and visible focus.
- [ ] Targeted verification covers real `MainActivity` navigation through the Tasks screen, Room-backed date/list membership and priority ties, completion/reopening, quick-entry routing including renamed Inbox, edits and recurrence updates, a long/empty view, tab restoration/fallback, offline restart, and large-text/layout accessibility. Record actual commands, device/API, tested commit, evidence and unavailable checks in `_docs/task-20-validation.md`; independent QA checks each criterion before closure.
