## Goal

Delete individual tasks and independent notes with the owner-approved confirmation and recovery behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Task details and the note editor expose clear deletion actions for the currently opened item.
- [ ] Canceling deletion leaves the item and its relationships unchanged; confirmation or undo behavior exactly matches #3.
- [ ] Deleting a task removes its checklist and updates every applicable screen/search result; unrelated notes remain untouched.
- [ ] Deleting a recurring occurrence or series uses #19's explicit scope and does not silently delete other occurrences.
- [ ] Deleting a note updates Notes/Home/search, and any approved undo restores the correct text, formatting, identity, and ordering.
- [ ] Failed deletes and expired/unavailable recovery display clear outcomes without claiming the item was removed or restored.

## Out of scope

- Whole-list deletion is tracked in [#21](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/21).
- Recurrence-scope rules/controls is tracked in [#19](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/19).
- OS reminder cancellation integration is tracked in [#28](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/28).
- Backup restoration is tracked in [#33](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/33).

## Constraints

- Prerequisites: #17, #19, and #25; recovery/confirmation rules from #3 and relationship policy from #4.
- Files/scope: Task/note delete actions and transactional repository/recovery integration under app plus focused checks.
- Use Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, and Room/SQLite as applicable. Version one works offline without accounts; optional sync planning remains [#40](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/issues/40). Do not add unrequested features.
- Follow [AGENTS.md](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/AGENTS.md), [scope](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/plan.md), and [process](https://github.com/xiaodlb/TDMK/blob/codex/task-1-android-setup/_docs/process.md). Read _docs/behavior.md and _docs/data-model.md for agreed rules/contracts; required unresolved decisions block implementation. Read testing/design guides before relevant work when present and report missing guides.
