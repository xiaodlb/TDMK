## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The note-rules section states card ordering (including ties), recent-note count on Home, save timing, title/empty-body behavior, and concrete before/after examples for creating and editing a note, including a blank or whitespace-only note and leaving the editor.
- [ ] A search table defines matching and searchable fields for Home, Tasks, Notes, and Log, including Home coverage, the Tasks scope across views/lists, case/Unicode handling, blank queries, no matches, and completed-today inclusion. Concrete examples cover each tab, formatting-independent note text, and matching/nonmatching queries without changing the agreed per-tab navigation.
- [ ] A deletion table states confirmation and undo/recovery behavior (or explicitly no recovery) for individual tasks and notes, including canceling confirmation and any recovery availability/expiry. Concrete examples cover subtasks, completed tasks, recurring occurrences/series and their exclusions, and reminder cancellation/recovery; preserve #2's confirmed series/list deletion rules and protected default list.
- [ ] Backup rules state versioned format, included/excluded records and settings (including formatted notes, completion history, recurrence exceptions, and reminder state), export destination and export failure/cancellation, restore replacement or merge policy, unsupported versions, malformed/inconsistent files, restore cancellation/failure, and the fate of existing data and reminder schedules. Concrete examples cover a successful restore and rejected/canceled restore with no partial data replacement; preserve #2's no-duplicate occurrence and no-replayed-reminder rules.
- [ ] Every policy needed by downstream implementation has a concrete example and recorded source: an explicit owner selection or a choice made under the owner's 2026-10-12 delegation. The decision status identifies all four topic groups as resolved before handoff to QA; routine preferences are decided autonomously and are not presented as individually owner-approved.
- [ ] The decisions retain independent notes with titles and bold/headings/bullets, a two-column grid, a full-screen editor, per-tab search, offline backup/restore without an account, and deferred cloud sync; prior explicit choices and #2 task policies remain unchanged.
