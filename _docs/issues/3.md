## Goal

Resolve note, search, deletion-recovery, and backup choices and record them in _docs/behavior.md without changing already agreed features.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The note-rules section states card ordering, recent-note count on Home, save timing, title/empty-body behavior, and examples showing the result of editing or creating a note.
- [ ] A search table defines matching and fields for Home, Tasks, Notes, and Log, including Home coverage, case/Unicode handling, blank queries, no matches, and whether completed-today items are included.
- [ ] A deletion table states confirmation and undo/recovery behavior for individual tasks and notes, plus how recovery interacts with subtasks and recurring occurrences; list deletion still requires confirmation and deletes its tasks.
- [ ] Backup rules state versioned format, included records/settings, export destination, restore replacement or merge policy, unsupported versions, malformed files, cancellation, and the fate of existing data and reminder schedules.
- [ ] Unresolved choices are asked one at a time and every implementation-blocking rule records the owner's approval rather than an assumed answer.
- [ ] The decisions retain independent notes, bold/headings/bullets, a two-column grid, a full-screen editor, offline use, and deferred cloud sync.
